System Objectives

Designed for secure payment tracking and transparent clearance monitoring

The system improves the management of student payments, clearance requests, department approvals, and real-time communication between students, parents, and school personnel.

payments

Centralized Payment Records

Records and manages student payment transactions in a centralized database to ensure financial accuracy and eliminate manual errors.

Secure Online Portal

Online Payments & PayMongo

Supports seamless digital payments via QR Ph, GCash, Maya, and cards through PayMongo gateway integration.

Digital Clearance Requests

Allows digital submission and verification of subject teacher clearance requests with multi-assignment matching.

Sequential Department Approvals

monitoring

Real-time Status & Analytics

Provides real-time clearance progress monitoring and financial collection summaries for school administrators.

Simple Step-by-Step Process

A smoother process for students, parents, and school personnel

From payment recording to clearance approval, the platform organizes each step into a clear digital workflow that is easier to monitor, verify, and update.

1

Student Sign In

Access balance details, net assessments, payment due dates, and subject clearance status.

2

Payment Allocation

Partial payments are automatically allocated to the student's oldest unpaid installment due schedule.

3

Clearance Approvals

Assigned subject teachers and department heads review and approve digital clearance requests.

4

Certificate Download

After all approvals are cleared, students download their official Certificate of Clearance.
